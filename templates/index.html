ABOUT SEVACONNECT

Creating Hope, Changing Lives

SevaConnect is a social welfare platform created to connect volunteers, donors and communities for meaningful social initiatives.

We believe that every individual has the power to contribute towards a better society. Through education, healthcare, environmental activities and community support, we work to create positive change.
